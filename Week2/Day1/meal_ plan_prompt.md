## C — CONTEXT & NON-NEGOTIABLE GUARDRAILS
1. **100% Vegetarian & Authentically South Indian:**
   - Every cell must name real, recognizable South Indian dishes (e.g., *steamed idli, moong dal pesarattu, ragi dosa, ven pongal, vegetable rava/broken-wheat upma, hand-pounded/parboiled rice, arachuvitta sambar, milagu rasam, chow-chow kootu, beans/cabbage poriyal, avial, mor kuzhambu, kala chana sundal, spiced buttermilk*).
   - **Strictly forbidden:** Vague labels (never write *"healthy breakfast"* or *"mixed veg curry"*) and non-South Indian trendy substitutes (no quinoa, avocado, kale, chia pudding, or overnight oat bowls).
2. **Zero Guessing on Allergies, Dairy & Oil:**
   - Audit every component—including tempering (*tadka*), chutneys, and snacks—against **Allergies & strict exclusions** and **Dietary style**.
   - If nut-free is specified, eliminate peanut chutney and cashew garnishes completely; substitute with roasted gram (*pottukadalai*), tomato-onion, or mint-coriander chutney.
   - Keep added fats measured and modest (e.g., `1–2 tsp cooking oil/ghee per meal`, omitting ghee if dairy-free).
3. **Weekday Time & Household Realism:**
   - Weekday meals must fit within the **Weekday cooking time limit** by using realistic home habits (pre-fermented idli/dosa batter, soaked pulses, one-pot sambar/kootu, or repurposing lunch poriyal/sambar smartly).
   - Keep dishes family-friendly so everyone listed in **Household eaters** can eat the same meal.
4. **Balanced Weight-Loss Nutrition (No Extreme Restriction):**
   - Include complex carbs, plant proteins (toor/moong/urad/chana dal, legumes, or curd/buttermilk if allowed), and high-fibre vegetables daily in ordinary, sustainable portions. No starvation calories or meal skipping.
